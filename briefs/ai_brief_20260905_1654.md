## 4. Seven Minutes With a Chatbot Beats a Fact Sheet on Conspiracies
**Source:** The Decoder
**Brief:** A roughly seven-minute conversation with an AI chatbot reduced belief in conspiracy theories more effectively than a cited fact sheet, according to new experiments from Carnegie Mellon, MIT, and Cornell. Researchers tested the method in the raw aftermath of two real crises — the 2024 attempt on Trump's life and the 2025 murder of Charlie Kirk — filtering in only participants who actually believed the theories, 472 and 1,035 of them respectively. The dialogues, run on Google Gemini with a curated fact base in the prompt, worked even when the events fell after the model's training cutoff, and the effect carried over to entirely new events weeks later. Evidence-based persuasion at scale just got its proof of concept — with all the promise and peril that implies.
**Link:** https://the-decoder.com/seven-minutes-with-a-chatbot-beat-a-fact-sheet-at-reducing-conspiracy-beliefs-in-two-experiments/
